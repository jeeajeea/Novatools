    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about our productivity tools

    Is my data saved automatically?

    Yes! All your data including to-do items, flashcard decks, habit tracking, and settings are automatically saved to your browser's local storage. You can close the tab and return later with all your data intact.

    Can I export my data?

    Currently, data is stored locally in your browser. You can access it by going to your browser's local storage or by simply copying your content. We're working on adding export features in future updates.

    How does the Pomodoro timer work?

    The Pomodoro Technique uses 25-minute work sessions followed by 5-minute breaks. After 4 sessions, take a longer 15-30 minute break. Our timer tracks your sessions and lets you customize work and break durations.

    Can I create multiple flashcard decks?

    Yes! You can create multiple decks for different subjects. Use the "New Deck" button to create a new deck, and switch between them using the dropdown menu. Each deck stores its own set of flashcards.

    Does the habit tracker work offline?

    Yes, the habit tracker works completely offline. Once the page loads, you can track habits without internet. Your streaks and history are saved locally in your browser.

    How does the word counter work?

    Simply paste or type your text in the text area, and the word counter instantly displays word count, character count, sentence count, paragraph count, and estimated reading time. It also shows top keywords in your text.
