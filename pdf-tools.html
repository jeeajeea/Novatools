Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our PDF tools

Is my data secure?

Yes, absolutely! All PDF processing happens directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to any server, meaning your documents never leave your device. This makes our tools 100% private and secure.

What file formats are supported?

Our PDF tools support PDF files up to 50MB. For image conversion tools, we support PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG formats. Text files (.txt) can also be converted to PDF. All processing is done locally in your browser.

Can I merge multiple PDFs into one?

Yes! Our Merge PDF tool allows you to combine multiple PDF files into a single document. Simply drag and drop your files, arrange them in the desired order, and click merge. There's no limit on the number of files you can merge.

How does PDF compression work?

Our compression tool uses advanced algorithms to reduce PDF file size while maintaining document quality. You can choose between three compression levels: Low (best quality), Medium (balanced), and High (smallest file size). The compression happens entirely in your browser.

Can I rotate specific pages in a PDF?

Yes! Our Rotate PDF tool lets you rotate individual pages or all pages at 90, 180, or 270 degrees. Simply upload your PDF, select the pages you want to rotate, choose your rotation angle, and download the result.

Are these tools free to use?

Yes, all our PDF tools are completely free with no hidden costs. No signup required, no subscription, and no limitations on usage. We believe in making document tools accessible to everyone.
