What Are NovaTools PDF Tools?

Free, private, browser-based PDF tools that run entirely on your device

NovaTools offers a complete suite of free PDF tools that run entirely in your browser. From merging and compressing PDFs to converting images and adding signatures, every tool works locally on your device — your files never leave your computer.

Unlike online PDF services that require uploading files to remote servers, NovaTools processes everything using client-side JavaScript. Your sensitive documents stay completely private, and you can use the tools even without an internet connection once the page has loaded.

Whether you need to merge multiple PDFs, compress a large file, extract specific pages, or add a signature, NovaTools has you covered — all for free with no signup required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about NovaTools PDF tools

Is my data secure when using PDF tools?

Yes, absolutely! All PDF processing happens directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to any server, meaning your documents never leave your device. This makes our tools 100% private and secure.

What file formats are supported?

Our PDF tools support PDF files up to 50MB. For image conversion tools, we support PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG formats. Text files (.txt) can also be converted to PDF.

Can I merge multiple PDFs into one?

Yes! Our Merge PDF tool allows you to combine multiple PDF files into a single document. Simply drag and drop your files, arrange them in the desired order, and click merge. There's no limit on the number of files you can merge.

How does PDF compression work?

Our compression tool reduces PDF file size by re-rendering pages as optimized images. You can choose between three compression levels: Low (best quality), Medium (balanced), and High (smallest file size). All processing happens entirely in your browser.

Can I rotate specific pages in a PDF?

Yes! Our Rotate PDF tool lets you rotate individual pages or all pages at 90, 180, or 270 degrees. Simply upload your PDF, select the pages you want to rotate, choose your rotation angle, and download the result.

Are these tools really free to use?

Yes, all PDF tools are completely free with no hidden costs. No signup required, no subscription, and no limitations on usage. We believe in making document tools accessible to everyone.
