Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our calculators

Are these calculators accurate?

Yes, our calculators use standard mathematical formulas and algorithms to ensure accurate results. However, always double-check important calculations as we're not responsible for any errors in critical applications.

Is my data stored or tracked?

No, we don't store or track any of your calculations. All data stays in your browser and is never sent to any server. Your privacy is fully protected.

Can I save my GPA calculation?

Yes! The GPA calculator has a Save button that stores your courses and grades locally in your browser. You can return later and your data will still be there (as long as you don't clear your browser cache).

What does BMI category mean?

BMI categories are: Underweight (below 18.5), Normal (18.5-24.9), Overweight (25-29.9), and Obese (30 and above). Note that BMI doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or overall body composition.

How is compound interest calculated?

We use the standard compound interest formula: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is principal, r is annual rate, n is compounding frequency per year, and t is time in years. You can choose from various compounding frequencies including daily, monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, and annually.

Do I need to create an account?

No account required! All our calculators are free to use with no signup. Just open the page and start calculating immediately.
