Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

NovaTools uses privacy-friendly Google Analytics to collect anonymous aggregate data about site usage. This includes:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Browser type and device category
  • Approximate geographic region (city-level)

This data is anonymized (IP addresses are masked) and cannot be used to identify individual visitors. We use this information solely to understand how the site is used and to improve our services.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal documents or files
  • No uploaded content
  • No personal identification information
  • No cookies for tracking purposes

How Your Data Is Processed

All tool processing happens entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to any server. This means NovaTools cannot access, store, or share any documents you work with.

Third-Party Services

We use Cloudflare CDN for content delivery and Google Fonts for typography. These services may process your IP address as part of standard web operations.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at our contact page.
